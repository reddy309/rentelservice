PROPERTY MANAGEMENT

Our Rent Collection Process

We simplify rental income management with a structured, transparent process that keeps tenants informed and property owners up to date.

01. Rent Schedule Setup

We establish monthly rent amounts, payment due dates, lease terms and accepted payment methods for every unit.

02. Tenant Reminders

Tenants receive timely reminders before the due date, helping reduce missed payments and confusion.

03. Rent Payment Collection

Payments are collected through the agreed methods, such as bank transfer or a supported online payment service.

04. Payment Verification

Each payment is checked against the tenant account. Confirmed transactions are recorded and balances updated.

05. Receipts & Records

We maintain payment receipts, transaction details, outstanding balances and a clear rental payment history.

06. Owner Reporting

Owners can review collected rent, overdue amounts and monthly rental income through organized reports.

PROFESSIONAL TENANT MANAGEMENT

Our Tenant Screening Process

We help property owners identify suitable tenants through a consistent screening process focused on application accuracy, rental history, financial suitability and verified information.

01. Application Review

Collect rental applications and review the information provided by prospective tenants.

02. Identity Verification

Verify identity and supporting documents using appropriate, lawful verification procedures.

03. Income Assessment

Assess documented income and affordability against the property's rental requirements.

04. Rental History Check

Review available rental references and previous tenancy records, with appropriate consent.

05. Background Checks

Conduct legally permitted checks where applicable, with required consent and privacy safeguards.

06. Final Tenant Selection

Evaluate applications against consistent criteria and present suitable candidates to the property owner.
