PRICING FAQS

Questions About Our Pricing

Find answers to common questions about our property management plans, service charges, and payments.

Each plan includes specific property management services such as tenant coordination, rent tracking, maintenance support, and property reporting, depending on your selected package.

Listed prices are indicative monthly rates. Final charges may depend on property type, location, selected services, and the agreed service terms.

Repair materials, labour, and third-party service charges may be separate from the monthly management fee. Applicable costs should be confirmed before repair work begins.

You can contact our team to discuss upgrading or changing your plan. Changes depend on service availability and your agreed management terms.

Our displayed plans use monthly pricing. Your billing date, payment method, renewal process, and applicable taxes should be confirmed in your service agreement.

Additional charges may apply to services outside your selected plan, including certain repairs, materials, or third-party services. Applicable fees should be explained before approval.

Compare the services offered in each package. Consider your tenant management, rent tracking, reporting, maintenance coordination, and owner support requirements before choosing a plan.

Review the available plans and select the package that suits your property. Contact our team to discuss your requirements and confirm the pricing, included services, and next steps.
